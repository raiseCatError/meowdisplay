Product facts

Your Mac, on another screen.

One line

MeowDisplay is a free, open-source app that lets you view and control your Mac from an iPhone or iPad.

Short description

Mirror your Mac, extend its desktop, or arrange individual Mac windows on your iPhone or iPad. MeowDisplay combines touch control, keyboard input and optional system audio over USB, local Wi-Fi, or a reachable private network such as Tailscale.

Window view arranges individual Mac windows in panes. Stage Peek helps you switch between them. Controls and feature availability differ by platform.
