# Asset guidelines

Use the supplied artwork without redrawing, recolouring or distorting it. PNG files are authentic repository assets, not newly invented logos. No genuine vector source or separate dark/light logo variants have been verified, so none is supplied.

Official screenshots must be real captures of released MeowDisplay with harmless demonstration content. Promotional graphics are separate compositions and must be labelled as such. Do not imply they are raw screenshots. Keep captions clear about device and mode.

No video clips are included yet. Real live-session recordings can be added after capture and privacy review.
