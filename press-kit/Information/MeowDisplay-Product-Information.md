# MeowDisplay

## One line
MeowDisplay is a free, open-source app that lets you view and control your Mac from an iPhone or iPad.

## Short description
Mirror your Mac, extend its desktop, or arrange individual Mac windows on your iPhone or iPad. MeowDisplay combines touch control, keyboard input and optional system audio over USB, local Wi-Fi, or a reachable private network such as Tailscale. Mac and iPhone/iPad 2.0 are released. Android remains in closed testing.

## Longer description
MeowDisplay brings your Mac to another screen. Mirror an existing display, extend your desktop, or choose individual Mac windows in Window view. Arrange those windows in panes, and use Stage Peek to switch between them. Touch controls, keyboard input with a typing strip, and optional Mac system audio support everyday use from an iPhone or iPad.

Connect locally over USB or Wi-Fi. Remote Access requires a reachable private-network route that you provide, such as Tailscale, and trusted pairing. MeowDisplay does not operate an account service or cloud relay. Availability and controls differ by platform; Android is in closed testing and should not be presented as having full Apple-platform parity.

MeowDisplay is free and open source under GPL-3.0. Mac and iPhone/iPad version 2.0 are released. The Mac companion can share and receive; a separate receiver-only Mac app is also available.

- iPhone/iPad: iOS/iPadOS 16.4 or later
- Separate Mac receiver: macOS 12 or later

- Local connections: USB on iPhone/iPad, local Wi-Fi
- Remote Access: reachable private-network route, such as Tailscale; no MeowDisplay-hosted relay

## Core features on Apple mobile devices
- Mirror and Extend
- Window view with multiple panes
- Stage Peek window switching
- Touch and trackpad control, keyboard and typing strip
- Optional system audio
- Multiple receivers, with device-specific settings

Do not assume every feature is available on every platform. See the current setup and download pages for requirements.
