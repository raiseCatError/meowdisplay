# MeowDisplay 2.2: changes since 2.1 and QA checklist

This covers everything merged into `dev` for 2.2, from `v2.1.0` (2026-11-10) to the 2.2 integration
merge: about 300 commits, of which 44 are features and 140 are fixes. Items marked **(Beta)** are
behind a Beta label or switch in the app.

Automated status at the merge:
- iPhone Duo UI tests in Xcode Cloud: 34 run, 29 passed, 5 skipped because they need a live Mac,
  0 failed (Build 8).
- GitHub required checks (`mac`, `conventional`, `release-tooling`, `android`) passed.
- Not covered by automation: a live Mac session, a physical iPad, a physical Duo, more than one
  Mac display, and Android on a device. The checklist below covers those.

## Part 1: What changed from 2.1 to 2.2

### Window mode and Trackpad workspaces (iPhone and iPad)
- **Trackpad tab:** the original full-screen trackpad. Its border follows the display's rounded
  corners, the dot grid stays inside the border, a soft trail follows the finger, and a tap shows a
  press.
- **Video Off Trackpad:** a separate Trackpad for sessions with video off.
- **Extended display tab:** a session-owned tab for the extended display.
- **Overview pane / tab:** every Mac display live in one pane, controlled like a trackpad. It can be
  added from Displays and stays alongside existing tabs. It zooms and pans freely and asks for
  sharper stills when zoomed in.
- **Real Mac pointer in Full Desktop panes:** the pointer is marked in the pane and fades with the
  controls tray. Other panes show a smaller mark.
- **Pane menus:** the app menu bar's chevron (`<` / `>`) sits on the pane's right edge. Menus fit
  above any toolbar under them.
- **Floating panes:** the close dot appears only on the selected float, at its top-left corner. The
  title pill no longer moves.
- **Auto-hide:** workspace controls fade with the controls tray. Pane tabs stay visible.
- Show minimized windows asks for control first. Restoring a listed window no longer switches the
  display mode. Mission Control is told apart from unavailable windows.

### Display Overview and arrangement (Beta)
- **Full-screen display overview:** every display, with the Mac cursor shown. Fit All, pan, pinch
  and double-tap to a display.
- **Arrangement map** in the Stage Peek gallery, with one Displays header holding **Arrange (Beta)…**
  and **Overview (Beta)**.
- **Arrange (Beta):** edit the Mac's display arrangement from the phone or tablet. The Mac asks its
  owner every time, the change can be reverted, and approval is scoped to the paired device.
- **Identify Displays.** (Move Cursor Here was removed.)

### Cursor
- **Off-screen cursor locator (opt-in):** points to a Mac cursor that is on another display or over
  no pane in Window mode. A re-entry marker shows where it comes back.
- Receiver input stays on its own display. View Only shows the real cursor.

### Controls
- **Arrow keypad:** modifiers toggle on touch, full-size keys, a key preview, and the modifier row
  as one segmented capsule.
- **New shortcuts:** Command-T (New Tab) in the default Command palette, and Command-Q, added
  carefully to existing palettes.
- **Roomy iPhone Duo windows** get iPad-style controls. The compact tray keeps clear of a fold.
- Canceled arrow holds are released before another repeat.
- **Accessibility:** auto-hidden controls leave the accessibility tree, so VoiceOver no longer
  reaches invisible controls.

### Sleep, lock screen and wake
- **Asleep cover:** says when the Mac is asleep, offers **Wake Mac** on a live session, and has
  Settings and Back to Home (also before the first picture).
- **Lock screen:** a fresh lock screen picture while locked, Show Lock Screen can time out or be
  cancelled, and new connections are told the Mac is locked.
- **Wake and connect:** sends the wake when the peer identity arrives late, asks the Mac to dial
  again after a dropped session, and says when no wake packet can be sent. Only an explicitly
  requested session wakes the Mac at start.
- Mirror starts once when the display returns. Extend and Window mode wait for a waking display
  instead of failing.

### Mac
- **Stay Awake with Lid Closed (optional):** with careful ownership of the sleep setting, and it
  waits for authorization before an ordinary Quit.
- **Desktop Size** for larger text on the extended desktop (also in the Mac Receiver). The virtual
  display declares a realistic physical size.
- **Thunderbolt Mac-to-Mac:** sessions over a Thunderbolt cable are labelled Thunderbolt.
- Clearer pairing approval guidance, and an explanation when pairing is refused.
- Hardening: bounds on hello geometry, scroll, Pencil pressure and clipboard counters (these fixed
  remote crashes in shipped builds).

### Streaming and data use
- **Use Less Data** (Automatic / On / Off) on iOS and the Mac Receiver, with a one-time suggestion
  on metered networks.
- **Adaptive Automatic (Beta switch on the Mac):** adjusts quality and refresh to the network.
- Receiver resync and stale-frame keyframe requests, and audio guards (format changes, unusable
  sample rates and channel counts, empty payloads).

### Diagnostics (developer)
- Opt-in streaming diagnostics with a developer view, stage table, verdict and redacted export.
  Colour metadata checks.
- Performance Lab for measured comparisons (developer tool).

### Stage Peek, Home and other screens
- **All Apps** covers the whole peek while open. Minimized window actions in every layout. Mirror
  sources can be picked straight from Stage Peek.
- **Home:** the kept-session card says when clipboard sharing is still on.
- Stage Peek buttons, the Video Off screen and Settings pickers adapt to large text and narrow
  windows.
- **Demo mode:** the calculator, pane menus and toolbar keep clear of the demo's own chrome and the
  controls.
- The app list finds apps on external drives and behind links.

### Android
- Window view chrome stays on the picture while it eases.
- Shows the Mac's Streaming Mode and Custom frame rate.

## Part 2: QA checklist

Install the QA candidate side by side with the release apps (MeowDisplay QA on iOS, MeowDisplay
Dev on the Mac). Tick each item on every device you have. Mark anything odd with the device,
orientation and a screenshot.

### Devices
- [ ] iPhone (portrait and landscape)
- [ ] iPad (portrait and landscape)
- [ ] iPhone Duo, if available: folded, half open and open
- [ ] Mac with two or more displays (for Overview and Arrange)
- [ ] Android, if available

### 1. Install and connect
- [ ] The QA apps install next to the release apps, and both launch.
- [ ] Pairing works. A refused pairing explains why.
- [ ] Connect over Wi-Fi. Connect over USB. On a Mac-to-Mac Thunderbolt cable, the route says
      Thunderbolt.
- [ ] Mirror, Extend and Window mode each start, and switching between them works.

### 2. Controls tray and Auto-hide
- [ ] Turn on Auto-hide. After the timeout, the tray, the app menu chevron and the workspace
      controls fade out. Pane tabs stay visible.
- [ ] A touch brings everything back, and the touch also reaches the Mac.
- [ ] Tap the gear after a reveal: Settings opens. Hold the gear: Session Actions opens.
- [ ] With VoiceOver on, hidden controls can't be reached. They come back after a reveal.
- [ ] Arrow keypad: modifiers toggle, keys are full size, the key preview shows, and holds stop
      when cancelled.
- [ ] Command-T opens a new tab. Command-Q works and existing palettes still have their own keys.
- [ ] On a Duo, the tray keeps clear of the fold in every posture.

### 3. Window mode
- [ ] Add panes, float a pane, rearrange, and remove from layout.
- [ ] The selected float shows its close dot on its top-left corner only. The title pill stays put.
- [ ] The app menu chevron is on the pane's right side. `<` opens the menus, `>` folds them.
- [ ] Pane menus show every item, never under a toolbar or the controls.
- [ ] In a Full Desktop pane, the real Mac pointer is marked. Other panes show a smaller mark. The
      marks fade when idle.
- [ ] Show minimized windows asks for control first. Restoring a window doesn't change the display
      mode.

### 4. Trackpad
- [ ] Trackpad tab: the border meets the display edges and curves at the corners. No dots outside
      the border.
- [ ] A soft trail follows the finger, and a tap shows a press.
- [ ] With video off, the Video Off Trackpad works.
- [ ] An Apple trackpad moves the pointer at the expected speed.

### 5. Display Overview and Arrange (Beta), two or more Mac displays
- [ ] Displays header: Arrange (Beta)… and Overview (Beta) on one line, or on their own row when
      narrow, never wrapped mid-word.
- [ ] Overview: Fit All, pan, pinch, double-tap a display. The Mac cursor shows. Zoomed views
      sharpen.
- [ ] Identify Displays shows each display's name on the Mac.
- [ ] Arrange: the Mac asks first. Keep keeps it, Revert puts it back, and a second arrangement
      after Keep works.
- [ ] Add Overview as a pane. It stays alongside existing tabs.

### 6. Cursor locator (opt-in)
- [ ] Turn it on in Settings. Move the Mac cursor to another display: the locator points to it.
- [ ] Bring the cursor back: the re-entry marker shows where.
- [ ] In View Only, the real cursor shows.

### 7. Sleep, lock and wake
- [ ] Put the Mac to sleep during a session: the asleep cover shows, with Wake Mac, Settings and
      Back to Home.
- [ ] Wake Mac works. Mirror, Extend and Window mode come back without failing.
- [ ] Lock the Mac: the lock screen picture is fresh, and Show Lock Screen can be cancelled.
- [ ] Mac: turn on Stay Awake with Lid Closed, close the lid, and check the session stays up.
      Quit asks for authorization first when needed. Turn it off: normal sleep returns.

### 8. Streaming and data use
- [ ] Use Less Data: Automatic, On and Off each behave as labelled, on iOS and on the Mac
      Receiver. The suggestion shows only once on a metered network.
- [ ] Mac: turn the Adaptive Automatic Beta switch on and off. With it off, nothing restarts
      capture on its own.
- [ ] Audio plays and survives a change of output device.
- [ ] Desktop Size on an extended desktop makes text larger, also in the Mac Receiver.

### 9. Stage Peek, Home, Settings, Demo
- [ ] All Apps covers the whole peek and goes back cleanly.
- [ ] Mirror sources can be picked from Stage Peek.
- [ ] Home: the kept-session card mentions clipboard sharing when it's on.
- [ ] Settings: every page opens. Large text and narrow windows still fit.
- [ ] Demo mode: calculator, pane menus and the Window view toolbar don't overlap anything.

### 10. Android (if available)
- [ ] Window view chrome stays on the picture.
- [ ] Streaming Mode and Custom frame rate match the Mac.

### Sign-off
- [ ] No crashes. If one happens, send the crash log.
- [ ] Anything that blocks release is listed with device, steps and a screenshot.
